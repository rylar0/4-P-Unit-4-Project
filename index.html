My Top 3 Favorite Gachiakuta Episodes!

Episode 1: Tenkai and Episode 2: Yadori-mono

Gachiakuta is a a manga that was adapted into a show created by Kei Urana.
I think that these two episodes are amazing because they work together to establish the amazing worldbuilding of the story that is explored throughout the show.
The illustrations are also amazing since the creator combines her artstyle with a graffiti artist who brings the world to life.
It's a compelling start to an amazing story that I love so much!!!
